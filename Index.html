CBD-An Overview


Cannabidiol

CBD, scientifically known as cannabidiol is part of the cannabinoid family. It is derived from plants such as Cannabis sativa and Cannabis indica. From the raw Cannabis plants a variety of Phyto cannabinoids can be obtained, one of them being CBD. Subjecting the cannabinoid extract to specific processes yields CBD oil, which is later incorporated into various dosage forms.

Cannabinoid family Types

a)Phyto cannabinoids, which are derived from plants

b)Endocannabinoids, which are naturally present in humans

We, at Bené, make sure that the CBD oil is obtained via the carbon di oxide extraction process, which is the most efficient process and helps remove any and all harmful chemicals such as THC.

Endocannabinoids and their execution

In most mammals including humans, cats and dogs, there is a pre-existing mechanism to maintain homeostasis. The mechanism we are talking about is the Endocannabinoid System (ECS).
Endocannabinoid functions
  • Sleep – wake cycle
  • Appetite
  • Behavioural patterns
  • Mood
Few important endocannabinoids are 2-AG, EAE and anandamide.

The biology behind CBD action

  • Your pet’s nervous system has certain receptors which are in collaboration with the endocannabinoids.
  • Research has proved that these same receptors can also interact with Phyto cannabinoids such as CBD to bring satisfactory effects.
  • For instance, CBD can bind with the CB1 receptor in the brain and regulate sleeping and behavioural patterns. CBD can also bind with the CB2 receptor and help in reducing pain and inflammation.
  • Sometimes, CBD can also influence the activity of endocannabinoid, anandamide which takes up serotonin, the hormone responsible for elevating happiness levels.
